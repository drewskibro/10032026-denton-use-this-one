UNDERSTANDING HAIR LOSS

What Causes Hair Loss?

Hair loss affects millions of men and women. Understanding the cause is the first step towards effective treatment. Our pharmacists in Denton can help identify the right solution for you.

Male Pattern Baldness

The most common type of hair loss in men, caused by a genetic sensitivity to DHT (dihydrotestosterone). It typically starts with a receding hairline or thinning at the crown.

Hormonal Changes

Hormonal fluctuations due to pregnancy, menopause, thyroid conditions, or stress can trigger temporary or long-term hair thinning in both men and women.

Lifestyle & Nutrition

Poor diet, stress, lack of sleep, and nutritional deficiencies (particularly iron and vitamin D) can all contribute to hair thinning and loss over time.

Finasteride

Oral tablet - once daily

How It Works

Finasteride blocks the enzyme that converts testosterone to DHT, the hormone responsible for shrinking hair follicles. Clinical trials show it stops further hair loss in 90% of men.

Key Benefits

  • Stops hair loss in 9 out of 10 men
  • Promotes regrowth in many cases
  • Simple once-daily tablet
  • Clinically proven and MHRA approved

Minoxidil

Topical solution - applied twice daily

How It Works

Minoxidil is a topical treatment that stimulates blood flow to hair follicles, extending the growth phase and promoting thicker, stronger hair. Suitable for both men and women.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Hair Loss Treatment FAQs

Clinical studies show that Finasteride stops further hair loss in approximately 90% of men and promotes visible regrowth in around two-thirds of users. Results typically become noticeable after 3-6 months of consistent use.

Both Finasteride and Minoxidil are well-tolerated by most people. Finasteride may cause mild side effects in a small percentage of men, which typically resolve on stopping treatment. Our pharmacist will discuss all potential side effects during your consultation.

Minoxidil is suitable for women experiencing hair thinning. However, Finasteride is only licensed for use in men. Our pharmacists can assess your individual situation and recommend the most appropriate treatment during a consultation.

Most patients begin to notice reduced hair loss within 3 months and visible regrowth between 6-12 months. Minoxidil may show initial results slightly sooner. Consistency is key - these treatments work best with daily, long-term use.

Finasteride requires a prescription, which our pharmacists can issue following a consultation. Minoxidil is available over the counter without a prescription. Either way, we recommend a consultation so we can advise the best treatment for your needs.

Hair loss treatments need to be continued to maintain results. If you stop taking Finasteride or applying Minoxidil, hair loss will gradually resume over the following months. Our team will support you with an ongoing treatment plan.
